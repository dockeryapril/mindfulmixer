# Mindful Mixer audio credits

The app ships local, commercially usable recordings so playback works without
streaming. Each asset was converted to AAC-LC (`.m4a`), resampled to 48 kHz,
and loudness-normalized for a balanced mixer. Filenames below refer to the
processed files in this directory.

## Modifications

- All recordings were transcoded to AAC-LC at 96 kbps and normalized to
  approximately -24 LUFS with a -2 dB true-peak ceiling.
- The Om recording received a very short fade-in and three seconds of padding
  so repeated chants retain a calm pause.
- No source recording was generated by AI.
